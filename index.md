# Privacy Policy — English Fun! (English Kids)

_Last updated: 2026-05-31_

English Fun! ("the app") is an English-learning app for children. We designed it to be safe and private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

## The short version

**By default, the app works fully offline and collects no personal information.**
There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. A parent can
optionally turn on cloud backup from the Parent Area — and only then is game
progress backed up to our secure cloud. The child's name and chosen character
are NEVER uploaded; they stay on the device.

## Information stored on the device

The app saves the following **only on your device** (using the device's local storage). It never leaves the device and we never see it:

- The player profiles you create (a name and a chosen character).
- Game progress: which levels are unlocked, stars earned, and shop items.

You can remove all of this at any time by deleting a profile inside the app, or by deleting the app.

## Optional cloud backup (parent-enabled)

A parent can create an account in the **Parent Area** (Sign in with Apple or
email + password). Creating an account turns on cloud backup. When enabled:

- We back up **game progress only** — levels, stars, score, and shop items.
- We store the **parent's email** (an adult's, given with consent) to manage the
  account and send optional reports.
- **The child's name and character are never uploaded.** They remain on the
  device only.
- Data is stored with Supabase (our cloud provider) and protected by per-account
  access rules so only that account can read its data.

A parent can **delete the account and all cloud data at any time** from the
Parent Area ("מחיקת חשבון"). Deleting the app also removes all local data.

## Microphone & speech recognition

One game ("Say It" / "תגיד את זה") lets a child practice saying an English word out loud. When the child taps the microphone:

- The microphone is used **only** during that activity.
- Speech is processed **on the device** using Apple's built-in speech recognition.
- **No audio is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.** It is used in the moment only to check whether the word was said, and then discarded.

The microphone is never used outside this game.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for children and complies with Apple's Kids Category requirements and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K). Because the app collects no data, there is no personal information of children (or anyone) to access, use, or disclose. There are no behavioral ads and no third-party data sharing.

## No third parties

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social logins.
Optional cloud backup (above) uses Supabase solely to store a parent's account
and a child's game progress — never for advertising or tracking.
